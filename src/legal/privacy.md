This policy explains how Psychiatric Health Solution, P.L.L.C. ("we," "us") handles information related to this website. How we use and protect your health information as a patient is described separately in our Notice of Privacy Practices, which you'll receive when you begin care.

## Information this website collects

This website does not ask for or collect health information. There are no forms on this site for submitting personal or medical details.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical information when you visit, such as your browser type, the pages you view and an anonymized network address, to keep the site secure and working properly. We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not sell it.

## Booking and patient platforms

When you book or complete intake, you'll use secure third-party platforms such as Headway, Grow Therapy or our patient portal. Those platforms are designed for healthcare and handle your information under their own privacy policies and the agreements they hold with us. Please review their policies when you use them.

## Email and phone

Email is not a secure way to share health information. Please don't include diagnoses, medications or other health details in email. When you call the practice, our phone assistant may take your name, callback number and a short message so the care team can follow up.

## Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, such as our booking partners. We aren't responsible for their content or privacy practices.

## Children

This website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children through it.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
